## Story

It is a terminal game made using curses library in python. It has a game screen on right to display character and story text is displayed on left. The story is that you are invited to a halloween party by your friends. Sadly you are broke as always , you dont haven money to buy a creepy halloween custome.But it is not a normal halloween for you as you have just discovered your super nature ability to see ghostly world. There are specific spirtural points in your realm where the wall between two realm is weak. You have to use that point to enter ghostly realm. You have to do missions by catching spirits and earn coins so you can buy yourself a halloween costume.
